## Findings
- [P0] (leakage) The HTF take-profit target uses swing highs/lows that are **not yet confirmed** at decision time — `mtf_smc/strategy/context.py:96-115` (the gate at line 109 is `self.df.index[s.index] + self.dur > ts`, i.e. the close of the swing *bar*, not of bar `s.index + lookback`) — why it matters: a fractal swing at bar j is knowable only after bars j+1..j+k close (SPEC §2.1; `swings.py:3-4`). My truncation probe (`check_target_leak.py`) found **35/320 lookups differ** between the full series and the series truncated at `ts` (15 with k=2 `nearest_swing`, 20 with k=5 `major_swing`). The function feeds `entries.py:54` (HTF_level and scale_2R_then_HTF: 28 of the 42 grid configs, including the "+2.00 R gold" `cascade_W1_H4_M15_HTF_level` and "+1.05 R GBPJPY" `cascade_W1_H4_M5_HTF_level` cells, where on W1 the leak spans up to 5 weekly bars), `entries.py:230` (the L1 legacy_smc hybrid TP, D1, k=2) and `random_entry.py:178`. This contradicts README:88 "Proven zero look-ahead" and SPEC §3.3(b) "from already-confirmed structure". The direction of the bias is undetermined and the magnitude is not verified (needs data) — recommended fix: gate on the close of bar `s.index + k` (k = swing_lookback or major_swing_lookback), add a truncation test on `nearest_opposing_swing`, re-run the 5 grids and the L1 WF (M).
- [P0] (leakage) A pending limit is cancelled because of the **same bar's close** even when that bar already traded through the limit — `mtf_smc/engine/backtester.py:123-129` (the invalidation check on `c[i]` at lines 123-126 runs before the fill check at line 129) — why it matters: the fill would have happened intrabar, before the close was known. So bar-close information is used to skip trades that are very likely losers (price closed beyond the POI). `check_fillbar.py` case B: the bar trades through entry 2000, closes at 1989 below invalidation 1995, and no trade is recorded. The bias is optimistic, in the strategy's favour; the magnitude is not verified — recommended fix: resolve the fill first, and apply close-based invalidation only to orders the bar did not touch; re-run (S).
- [P0] (factual) The "141 passing tests, CI-verified" claim is false for CI — `README.md:13`, `README.md:31` — why it matters: without the licensed cache, CI can run only 132. The 9 skipped tests include the end-to-end no-look-ahead test (`tests/test_backtester_integration.py:25`), the real-data IS-slice test (`tests/test_loader_slice.py:34-43`), WF and random-entry. I got 132 passed / 9 skipped locally. The author's own commit `c04c7ab` message records "132 passed, 9 skipped" and also wrongly says "all 141 tests build synthetic data in-test" — recommended fix: say "141 tests; 132 run in CI, 9 need licensed data" (S).
- [P0] (factual) Three different numbers are given for the post-fix stop-out verification — `docs/REPORT_MULTI_ASSET.md:101` ("median real stop-out −1.14 to −1.23 R") vs `DESIGN_DECISIONS.md:57` ("−1.02 to −1.04 R") vs commit `fc6af2f` ("−1.06..−1.09R") — why it matters: the only code that computes this, `scripts/verify_instruments.py:41-46`, produces **one synthetic 50-pip stop-out per instrument**, not a median of real stop-outs. I reproduced EURUSD at −1.030. Commit `971c8eb` says no artifact produces −1.14/−1.23 but left the report unchanged — recommended fix: compute the median from real grid trades and commit it, or relabel it as a synthetic single-trade check; make the docs agree (S).
