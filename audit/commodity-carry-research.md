## Stats helpers inventory
- src/stats.py:30 `stationary_bootstrap_sharpe`: Politis–Romano stationary bootstrap. Geometric block length with mean 21 (p_restart=1/21), circular wrap, 10,000 reps, 95% percentile CI. Seed defaults to `config.BOOTSTRAP_SEEDS[0]`=7 (`np.random.default_rng`). Annualized ×√252 (config.py:44).
- src/stats.py:82 `stationary_bootstrap_multi_seed`: runs the above for seeds (7, 13, 31) from src/config.py:56 and returns all three.
- scripts/phase1c_primary_backtest.py:184 (inline): bootstrap p-value = share of bootstrap Sharpes ≤ 0. Percentile-based and not null-centred; feeds BH.
- src/stats.py:92 `deflated_sharpe_ratio`: Bailey–López de Prado. Per-period SE from the Mertens-style formula with optional skew/kurt (default 0, and the runner passes none). Expected max Sharpe via the Gumbel/Euler–Mascheroni approximation with N_trials=14. Cross-trial dispersion defaults to the Sharpe SE.
- src/stats.py:147 `benjamini_hochberg`: step-up BH, q=0.10 (config.py:58).
- src/stats.py:168 `newey_west_tstat`: statsmodels OLS on a constant with a HAC covariance. Lag defaults to floor(4(n/100)^(2/9)); the premise pins it to 3 (src/premise.py:27).
- src/stats.py:183 `clustered_regression`: statsmodels OLS with SEs clustered by month (TS premise).
- src/premise.py:30 `monthly_spearman_ic`: per-month `scipy.stats.spearmanr`, needs ≥3 symbols.
- src/vol.py:34 `rolling_volatility`: 60-row rolling std (ddof=1, min_periods=60) ×√252. Applied to a 6-row-per-week calendar.
- scripts/phase1c_robustness.py:80 `sharpe_point` and scripts/generate_readme_figures.py:149 `sharpe_point`: duplicated mean/std(ddof=1)×√252.
- Not present: permutation test, PBO/CSCV, block bootstrap for variants, Lo/Newey-West Sharpe SE.
