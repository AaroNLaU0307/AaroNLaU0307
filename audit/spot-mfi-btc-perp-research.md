## Verified OK

- Backtest has no look-ahead: pos = target.shift(1) and return = open_{t+1}/open_t − 1 at src/backtest.py:47-48. MFI(D) uses the close of D and is first held from open D+1. Pinned by tests/test_backtest.py:26-39.
